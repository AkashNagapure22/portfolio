verified Numismatic Guides & Authentication: Identifying Counterfeit Coins

With high-value numismatic collectibles becoming targets for sophisticated counterfeiting, collectors must systematically cross-examine pieces using professional inspection protocols:

1. Weight & Dimensions Verification

Always use a calibrated milligram digital scale and precision digital calipers. Counterfeits cast from lighter base metal alloys or heavier lead/tin mixes will deviate outside official mint weight tolerances (usually within ±0.02g to ±0.1g).

2. Surface Texture & Micro-Detailing

Examine under 10x–20x magnification. Real struck coins exhibit sharp letter edges and clean metal flow lines. Counterfeits made via sand-casting or low-pressure injection often display granular pitting, micro-bubbles, or soft, rounded relief features.

3. Edge Reeding & Lettering Check

The edge is the hardest element for counterfeiters to replicate. Inspect reeding grooves for uniform depth and spacing. Missing or unevenly spaced edge lettering points directly to a fake or altered piece.

4. Magnetic & Sound Tests

Precious metals (Gold, Silver, pure Copper) are non-magnetic. If a coin reacts to a strong rare-earth magnet, it contains steel or nickel core plating. Authentic silver coins also emit a distinct, high-pitched lasting ring when balanced on a fingertip and gently struck.

factory Indian Mint Symbols & Location Guide

Mint marks indicate the specific facility where an Indian coin was manufactured. Here is the complete register of Indian government mints and their official symbols:

Mint Location Active Period / Notes Official Mint Mark Symbol
Mumbai (Bombay) Historical & Modern ◆ (Diamond) or B or M
Kolkata (Calcutta) Oldest operating mint in India No Mint Mark (Plain)
Hyderabad Nizam era & Republic issues ★ (Five-pointed Star), H, or Split Diamond
Noida Established 1988 (Modern Republic) ● (Small Solid Dot / Circle)
Foreign Mints (Contract) Issued during domestic shortages (e.g., Seoul, Heaton, Mexico City) M (Seoul), H (Birmingham/Heaton)

NUMISMATIC GUIDES & AUTHENTICATION

Determining Face Value vs. Market Value

  • 1. Intrinsic / Melt Value Spot value of precious metal content (silver, gold, bronze) based on current commodity markets.
  • 2. Numismatic Premium Collector demand, historical significance, rarity, and overall preservation grade that drive market worth.
  • 3. Sheldon Scale Grading Professional certified grading from Poor (P-1) to Mint State Perfect (MS-70).

Detecting Counterfeits & Mint Errors

  • 1. Precision Weight & Calipers Counterfeits deviate from official gram weight and edge thickness specifications.
  • 2. 10x Magnification Surface Inspection Look for casting bubbles, seam lines, or soft lettering typical of cast forgeries.
  • 3. Genuine Mint Errors Validate true die clashes, clipped planchets, and off-center strikes against post-mint damage.

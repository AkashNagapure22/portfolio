Determining Face Value vs. Market Value

  • price_change
    1. Intrinsic / Melt Value Spot value of the precious metal content (silver, gold, bronze) based on current commodity markets.
  • trending_up
    2. Numismatic Premium Collector demand, historical significance, rarity, and overall preservation grade that drive market worth far above face value.
  • grade
    3. Sheldon Scale Grading Professional grading from Poor (P-1) to Mint State Perfect (MS-70) that dictates certified market pricing.
verified_user

Detecting Fake Coins & Identifying Errors

  • balance
    1. Precision Weight & Dimensions Counterfeits often use wrong alloys leading to incorrect gram weight or thickness compared to official mint specifications. Always use a calibrated digital micro-scale.
  • search
    2. Magnification & Surface Texture Look for cast bubbles, soft or blurry lettering, and mold seams typical of fake cast coins, as opposed to crisp, struck mint details under 10x magnification.
  • error
    3. Genuine Mint Errors vs. Alterations Distinguish valuable genuine errors (e.g., clipped planchets, double strikes, off-center strikes) from post-mint damage or fraudulent alterations.
  • science
    4. Non-Destructive Testing Utilize acoustic ring testing for silver coins, magnetic checks (most vintage silver and copper coins are non-magnetic), and digital thickness calipers.

INDIAN MINT SYMBOLS, LOCATIONS & ESTABLISHMENT DATES

account_balance

Mumbai Mint

Established 1829

Location: Mumbai, Maharashtra

Mint Mark Symbols: Diamond ($\diamond$), Letter B, letter M, or sometimes no mint mark (for older issues).

One of India's oldest operational mints, under SPMCIL.

account_balance

Kolkata Mint

Established 1757

Location: Alipore, Kolkata, West Bengal

Mint Mark Symbols: No Mint Mark (blank space under the year) or letter C.

The historic first mint of British India, producing medals and circulation coinage.

account_balance

Hyderabad Mint

Established 1903

Location: Hyderabad, Telangana

Mint Mark Symbols: Split Diamond ($\diamond$), Five-pointed Star ($\star$), or letters H / M under the date.

Originally established under the Nizam of Hyderabad, later taken over by the Indian Government.

account_balance

Noida Mint

Established 1988

Location: Noida, Uttar Pradesh

Mint Mark Symbols: A small solid Round Dot / Circular mark under the date.

The most modern high-speed coin production facility established by the Government of India.

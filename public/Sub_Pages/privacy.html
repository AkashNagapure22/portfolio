Privacy & Cookie Policy

This portfolio is designed to respect minimal data collection, user privacy, and transparent technical communication.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the data needed to respond to direct technical inquiries, manage contact submissions, and preserve minimal local UI state for vote moderation and interaction persistence. We do not sell or lease user data.

2. Cookies and local storage

We may use browser local storage to remember vote state and session preferences. This helps prevent duplicate user voting on article interactions while keeping the experience fast and lightweight. No invasive tracking or third-party ad cookies are used on this site.

3. Use of data

Submitted contact details are used exclusively to respond to projects, collaboration inquiries, and technical discussions. We do not share those records with unrelated third parties.

4. Third-party services

Assets may be delivered through public CDNs and cloud-hosting services, and external links to LinkedIn or other platforms are governed by their own privacy policies.

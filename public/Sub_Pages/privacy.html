Privacy Policy

Learn how we protect your personal data across our portfolio websites.

1. Overview

This privacy policy explains how data is handled across the websites managed by Akash Nagapure. These sites include the main portfolio, technical blogs, and lab portals. We value your privacy and keep data collection to a strict minimum.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal details only when you choose to share them. This happens when you post a comment on a blog post or send a message through our contact terminal. The information we gather includes your name, email address, and message text.

We also store small text files called local storage data on your device. These files help remember your comment votes and site layout choices. They do not track your personal identity across other websites.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to reply to your messages, manage community discussions, and prevent comment spam. We never sell, trade, or rent your personal information to outside companies.

4. Third-Party Tools

Our web pages load standard code libraries from trusted content delivery networks, such as Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, and Google Fonts. Links to external platforms like LinkedIn follow the privacy rules set by those specific companies.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy or wish to remove your submitted comments, you can contact us directly by email at info@akashnagapure.in or akashnagapure@live.com.

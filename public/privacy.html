Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. We respect your data when you visit our website at https://www.akashnagapure.in. We only ask for info when we need it to help you. We collect data fairly with your consent and explain why we need it.

1. Information Collection & Web Server Logs

When you visit the site, our server may log basic details. This includes your IP address, browser type, operating system, visit dates, and requested pages. We use this data only to keep our site secure and running smoothly. We do not link IP addresses to personal user names.

2. Google Analytics & Usage Tracking

We use Google Analytics to study site traffic. It collects anonymous data to show how visitors use our pages. This helps us improve your reading experience. Google Analytics gathers info like your general region through cookies. It does not collect private names or phone numbers.

3. Cookies, Pixel Tags, and Local Storage

Cookies are small files saved on your computer. We use essential, performance, and analytical cookies to keep the site safe and remember your choices. Pixel tags help us track page visits. You can turn off cookies anytime in your web browser settings.

4. Third-Party Services & External Links

We keep your data only as long as needed. We never share your personal details with third parties unless required by law. Our site may link to outside web pages. We do not control those external sites and take no responsibility for their safety policies.

5. Policy Updates & Questions

We update this privacy policy from time to time as our labs grow. Please check back here to stay informed. If you have any questions about our privacy rules, please email us at info@akashnagapure.in.

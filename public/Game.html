MY REVIEWS

Black Myth: Wukong

9.0

A technical marvel that pushes the limits of Unreal Engine 5. The combat choreography is unmatched, though some level design feels linear.

PROS
  • Visual fidelity & Ray Tracing
  • Unbelievable boss variety
CONS
  • Occasional invisible walls
  • Frame rate optimization spikes

In-Depth Review & Breakdown

Black Myth: Wukong delivers one of the most visually breathtaking journeys through Chinese mythology ever created in gaming. Combat feels heavy yet fluid, rewarding fast-paced dodges and stance switches over passive blocking. Boss encounters are the core highlight, offering distinct animations and phase transitions that keep every chapter engaging.

Combat 9.5/10
Visuals 10/10
Story 8.0/10

Space Marine 2

8.5

Pure visceral adrenaline. Captures the Warhammer 40k scale perfectly. Coop is where the game truly shines.

PROS
  • Proprietary Swarm Engine tech
  • Epic scale of 40k battles
CONS
  • Slightly brief campaign
  • Initial server connectivity issues

In-Depth Review & Breakdown

Space Marine 2 brings back classic 7th-gen action gameplay elevated by next-gen rendering. Slaughtering thousands of Tyranids on screen with heavy bolters and chainswords feels punchy and immensely satisfying. The co-op Operations mode adds significant replay value beyond the campaign.

Gameplay 9.0/10
Graphics 9.0/10
Co-Op 9.5/10

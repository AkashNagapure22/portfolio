COLLECTION APPRAISALS

1911 King George V "Pig Rupee"

RARE

Infamous 1911 issue where the elephant on the King's chain resembled a pig, leading to public outcry and immediate withdrawal by the British mint.

PROS
  • High historical controversy value
  • Mintage withdrawn quickly
CONS
  • High forgery rates in local markets

Numismatic Breakdown

Due to the elephant design error on the order chain collar worn by King George V, religious sentiments were offended. The design was redesigned in 1912 with longer ears and a clearer trunk. Finding authentic 1911 pieces in Extremely Fine (XF) condition is a major triumph.

Historical Value 10/10
Rarity 9.5/10
Condition 9.0/10

Sher Shah Suri Silver Rupee (1540–1545)

KEY HISTORIC

The original coin that introduced the word "Rupiya" (Rupee) into the Indian monetary system, weighing standard 178 grains of pure silver.

PROS
  • Birth coin of Indian Rupee name
  • Clear Arabic & Nagari scripts
CONS
  • Often found with test marks

Numismatic Breakdown

Sher Shah Suri reformed the military and economic foundation of northern India during his brief reign. His silver Rupiya standardization was so robust that it was preserved by Akbar and later adopted by the British Empire as the official currency unit of the subcontinent.

Historical Impact 10/10
Calligraphy 9.0/10
Weight Std 10/10

Obverse Motif: Features 5 official imperial punch marks: Sun symbol of state authority, 6-arm symbol, 3-arched hill with crescent (Chaitya), elephant, and taurine/tree motif.

Reverse Motif: Blank or bears 1 small banker's test mark (shroff mark) applied during currency verification in ancient markets.

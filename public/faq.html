Getting Started

Welcome to Akash Nagapure's technical portfolio. This site documents enterprise endpoint management, cloud virtualization, and automation architectures built with Microsoft Intune, SCCM, VMware, and Azure. You can browse the technical blogs, review the main portfolio, or jump straight into the sections that matter to you.

Various versions have evolved

  1. Various versions of the lab have evolved across Intune, SCCM, and Azure.
  2. Discover the undoubtable value of progressive deployment rings and telemetry.
  3. Looked up one of the more obscure configuration patterns — and documented it.

How we can collaborate

Whether you need an architecture review, a migration strategy from SCCM to Intune, or hands-on guidance for Windows 365 / AVD rollouts, reach out through the contact email, the WhatsApp button on the main page, or the discussion sections on each page.

Ways to work together

  1. Enterprise Endpoint Management consulting (Intune, SCCM, MEM).
  2. Virtualization and Cloud VDI architecture (VMware, Citrix, AVD).
  3. Automation, scripting, and ITSM process hardening.

How to use the Blog

The Architecture Blog organizes every article by category (Intune, SCCM, Windows 365, AVD, VMware, PowerShell, Active Directory). Use the category filter pills to narrow the grid, or open the discussion section at the bottom of the page to ask questions about any article.

Pagination at the bottom of the blog grid lets you move between pages of articles without reloading the page.

How to subscription

Subscribe using the Stay Updated form in the footer of any page. Enter your email address and press Subscribe. You will be added to the newsletter list and notified whenever new projects or blog posts are published.

  • No spam — only project and blog announcements.
  • Unsubscribe anytime by contacting info@akashnagapure.in.
  • Your email is stored securely and never shared with third parties.

Properties

Each blog article includes a category badge, a publish date, and a short summary. The badge color indicates the technology family, so you can scan the grid quickly and find exactly the topic you are looking for.

How to set Design Style

The site is theme-oriented by default with a dark glass aesthetic. If you prefer to set your own reading style, use your browser's color/contrast settings, or drop a message in the discussion section with your feedback — the design is continuously refined based on visitor input.

How to use Mock up

The flip-card layouts throughout the site (projects, puzzles, labs) act as interactive mockups: click any card to flip it and reveal the step-by-step blueprint, architecture approach, or solution model on the back.

Preview

New articles and lab releases are previewed on the homepage and announced through the newsletter. Check the Changelog for the full release history and upcoming roadmap.

Still have questions? Use the discussion forms or write to info@akashnagapure.in.

CRITIC REVIEWS

Smoked Texas Beef Brisket

9.7

Smoked for 16 hours over post oak wood. The bark is jet black and crispy, yielding to melt-in-your-mouth rendering.

PROS
  • Deep smoke ring
  • Perfect fat render
CONS
  • Requires long wait times

Flavor Breakdown

The pepper-to-salt ratio on the rub is spot-on. Every bite balances deep hickory smoke with render-rich beef fat. Sauce is entirely optional because the meat carries itself effortlessly.

Tenderness 10/10
Smoke 9.5/10
Value 9.0/10

Authentic Thai Khao Soi

9.2

Northern Thai coconut curry noodle soup topped with crispy deep-fried egg noodles, pickled mustard greens, and lime.

PROS
  • Complex broth layers
  • Great texture interplay
CONS
  • Can be quite heavy

Flavor Breakdown

The sweetness of the coconut cream cuts through the sharp, earthy heat of dry red chilis and lemongrass. The contrast between soft boiled egg noodles inside and crunchy fried noodles on top makes it an unforgettable dish.

Broth 9.5/10
Aroma 9.0/10
Balance 9.0/10
